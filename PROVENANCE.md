# Source credits

DA-MLDG is based on [Vasileios Tsouvalas's repository](https://github.com/vtsouval/DA_MLDG),
source commit `8a95bc8003777f84a0cb2ea18efd9a53e929507d`.
The research authors are Raeshak King Gandhi, Vasileios Tsouvalas and Nirvana
Meratnia. Upstream files remain in `src/`; the installable `damldg/` package
provides data preparation, ResNet-18 training and second-order meta-learning.
